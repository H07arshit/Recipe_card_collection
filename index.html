Spaghetti

A quick and tasty pasta dish with tomato sauce.

Servings: 2

Ingredients

Click an ingredient to check it off

  • 200 g spaghetti
  • 300 g tomato sauce
  • 2 garlic clove
  • 20 g cheese

Steps

  1. Boil water and cook the spaghetti.
  2. Heat the tomato sauce with garlic in a pan.
  3. Mix the spaghetti with the sauce.
  4. Add cheese on top and serve hot.

Pancakes

Soft and fluffy pancakes for a sweet breakfast.

Servings: 2

Ingredients

Click an ingredient to check it off

  • 200 g flour
  • 2 egg
  • 240 ml milk
  • 2 tbsp sugar

Steps

  1. Mix flour, sugar, egg and milk into a smooth batter.
  2. Heat a pan and add a little butter.
  3. Pour the batter and cook until golden on both sides.
  4. Serve with honey or fruit.

Cheese Pizza

A simple homemade pizza with melted cheese.

Servings: 2

Ingredients

Click an ingredient to check it off

  • 2 pizza base
  • 6 tbsp pizza sauce
  • 100 g mozzarella cheese
  • 10 olives

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 200 degrees.
  2. Spread the sauce on the pizza base.
  3. Add the cheese and olives on top.
  4. Bake for 12 minutes and serve.
