Veg Noodles

Spicy stir-fried noodles with fresh vegetables.

Servings: 2

Ingredients

Click an ingredient to check it off

  • 200 g noodles
  • 50 g carrot
  • 50 g capsicum
  • 2 tsp soy sauce

Steps

  1. Boil the noodles and drain the water.
  2. Fry the chopped vegetables in a pan.
  3. Add the noodles and soy sauce.
  4. Mix well and serve hot.

Veg Burger

A juicy burger with fresh lettuce, tomato and cheese.

Servings: 2

Ingredients

Click an ingredient to check it off

  • 2 burger bun
  • 2 veg patty
  • 2 cheese slice
  • 4 tomato slices

Steps

  1. Cut the bun in half and toast it lightly.
  2. Cook the patty on a hot pan until brown.
  3. Place the patty, cheese and tomato on the bun.
  4. Close the bun and serve with sauce.

Hot Coffee

A warm and creamy cup of coffee to start your day.

Servings: 2

Ingredients

Click an ingredient to check it off

  • 4 tsp coffee powder
  • 4 tsp sugar
  • 200 ml milk
  • 100 ml water

Steps

  1. Boil the water and milk together.
  2. Put coffee powder and sugar in a cup.
  3. Pour the hot milk into the cup.
  4. Stir well and enjoy.

Veg Momos

Soft steamed dumplings filled with spicy vegetables.

Servings: 2

Ingredients

Click an ingredient to check it off

  • 200 g flour
  • 100 g cabbage
  • 50 g carrot
  • 2 garlic clove

Steps

  1. Make a soft dough with flour and water.
  2. Mix chopped cabbage, carrot and garlic for the filling.
  3. Roll the dough, add the filling and fold it.
  4. Steam for 10 minutes and serve with chutney.

Pancakes

Soft and fluffy pancakes for a sweet breakfast.

Servings: 2

Ingredients

Click an ingredient to check it off

  • 200 g flour
  • 2 egg
  • 240 ml milk
  • 2 tbsp sugar

Steps

  1. Mix flour, sugar, egg and milk into a smooth batter.
  2. Heat a pan and add a little butter.
  3. Pour the batter and cook until golden on both sides.
  4. Serve with honey or fruit.

Cheese Pizza

A simple homemade pizza with melted cheese.

Servings: 2

Ingredients

Click an ingredient to check it off

  • 2 pizza base
  • 6 tbsp pizza sauce
  • 100 g mozzarella cheese
  • 10 olives

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 200 degrees.
  2. Spread the sauce on the pizza base.
  3. Add the cheese and olives on top.
  4. Bake for 12 minutes and serve.
